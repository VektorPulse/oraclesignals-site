Event contracts (Kalshi)

  • A Kalshi contract pays $1 if its outcome happens and $0 if it doesn't. If you buy at 90¢ and lose, you lose the whole 90¢.
  • Favourites lose often enough to matter. One loss at a high price can wipe out many small wins: at 90¢ a loss costs about as much as 9 wins.
  • Kalshi charges fees. Prices move quickly, and some markets are thin, so you may not get the price in a call or pick.
  • Kalshi can pause, change or settle markets under its own rules, and its decisions are final for your account.

The bot (automatic trading in your own account)

  • In live mode, the bot places real orders in your Kalshi account, using the settings you choose. Real money is at risk.
  • Software fails. Bugs, server or internet outages, Kalshi or Telegram downtime and delays can cause missed trades, late trades, trades at bad prices or positions left open.
  • Your limits (trade size, daily stops) reduce the damage from a bad day, but they don't remove it.
  • Practice (paper) mode is simulated. It doesn't place real orders, so its results can be better than live trading would have been.
  • You can pause the bot with /pause and delete your key with /disconnect at any time. Watch your account.
