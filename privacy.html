Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What we collect

  • Payments: handled entirely by Whop. We never see or store your card details. Whop tells us your membership status and the Discord account you connected.
  • Discord: your Discord user ID and name, and which roles you have, so the right channels open for you.
  • Telegram bot (bot plans): your Telegram chat ID and name, your settings, and the trades the bot places for you. If you connect Kalshi, your API key is stored encrypted and is used only to place and manage orders on your account.
  • This website uses Whop's analytics script to count page views and to link visits to purchases on Whop, so we can see which pages and ads bring members. It may store an identifier in your browser. We don't use any other analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to give you access, deliver calls and picks, run the bot for you, and answer support requests, and to measure how visitors find us. We don't sell or rent your data.

Who else sees it

The services we run on: Whop (payments and website analytics), Discord, Telegram, Kalshi (only for your own orders, when you connect it) and our hosting provider. Each has its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting

You can disconnect the bot at any time, which deletes your Kalshi key. To have the rest of your data deleted, email us and we'll do it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
