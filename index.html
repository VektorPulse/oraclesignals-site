Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. Oracle Signals shares calls, picks and tools for education and entertainment. You decide what to trade or bet, and you can lose money.

Do I need a Kalshi account?

Only to trade the calls or use the bot with real money. Kalshi is a US exchange regulated by the CFTC; you open and fund your own account there. Oracle Signals is not affiliated with Kalshi.

Can the bot touch my money?

It places trades on your Kalshi account only within the limits you set, and it never withdraws money. Your Kalshi key is stored encrypted, and you can disconnect at any time, which deletes it.

How do I cancel?

In your Whop account, under your membership. Access lasts to the end of the period you paid for. See the refund policy.

How good are the results?

Every call and pick is graded in public in the Discord, with a daily and weekly record, losses included. Past results don't promise future ones.
