How a call is made

It watches every 15-minute market.

Kalshi lists a BTC and a DOGE market every quarter hour: will the price be higher when the window closes? The coach reads every one of them, around the clock.

It waits until its rules line up.

A call needs the favourite priced in its tested band with about 10 minutes left. Most windows never qualify, and no call is the right answer then.

It posts the call before the result.

The side, the price to pay and the time left go to Discord, with a heads-up first, so members can place it themselves or let the bot do it.

Then Kalshi grades it, win or loss.

Every call gets its stamp on the record. Losses stay on it too, so the record you see is the whole record.
